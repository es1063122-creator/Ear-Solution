Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Auris: Tinnitus Relief & Sleep (“Auris”, “the app”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the app.

Auris provides sound therapy, relaxation, sleep support, tinnitus frequency matching, desync sound, haptic-assisted routines, and personal tracking features. Auris is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

1. Information We Collect

Auris may process the following types of information:

  • App usage information: such as sound session usage, selected sound modes, routine progress, and app interaction data.
  • Device and technical information: such as device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data.
  • Advertising information: such as advertising identifiers and ad interaction data processed through Google AdMob.
  • Local tracking data: such as tinnitus level, sleep, stress, routine progress, and sound session records that may be stored on your device.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:
